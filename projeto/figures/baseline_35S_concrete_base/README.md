# Blender Baseline: 35S Radome and Concrete Base

This directory preserves the current reference scene before the next 3D expansion.

## Geometry

- Upper radome cut at latitude 35 degrees south;
- equator used as the radome mid-plane;
- reinforced-concrete parallelepiped base: 4 m x 4 m x 3 m;
- front service opening;
- triangular face on the outer shell;
- pyramid volume directed inward;
- support strut from the internal apex to the external VHF Yagi;
- Yagi boom as the continuation of the apex support axis;
- transverse parasitic elements;
- representative internal modules, FFASIC, timing core, service rings and optical/DC trunks.

## Known presentation issues for the next revision

1. World-space labels are difficult to read in perspective and should be replaced by camera-facing callouts or a separate annotation overlay.
2. The representative internal ribs, rings and modules are schematic and should be replaced by a coherent repeated structural system.
3. The next scene should instantiate identical triangular pyramid modules over the complete radome mesh.
4. Additional mechanical details, cable routing, thermal hardware and access structure can be added after the repeated-face assembly is stable.

The baseline files are intentionally kept together so later revisions can be compared against this exact scene.
