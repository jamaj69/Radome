\texttt{delay\_doppler} & Bistatic measurements and quality\\
\texttt{illuminator\_id} & Illuminator identity or hypothesis\\
\texttt{sync\_state} & locked, holdover or degraded\\
\texttt{environment} & Temperature, humidity and radome state\\
